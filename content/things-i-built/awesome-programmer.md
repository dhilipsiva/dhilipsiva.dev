A collection of resources to learn from and become a self-taught awesome programmer.

This one is personal. I'm a dropout — yes, I am not a graduate — and everything I know about building
software came from the open internet, shipped projects, and a terminal. This list (102★) is that path
written down: what to read, what to build, and in what order, for someone starting with nothing but
curiosity. If you need a certificate to succeed, you weren't educated properly; if you need a map, here's
mine.
