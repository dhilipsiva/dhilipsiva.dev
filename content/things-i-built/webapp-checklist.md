## Problem

Every web app launch trips over the same forgotten details — headers, backups, rate limits, the
unglamorous load-bearing parts.

## Approach

Write them all down, once, in public, and keep the list honest as the platform shifts.

## Outcome

~366 stars — the most-starred thing I've shipped, which proves the most useful artifact can be a list.
