## Problem

The Thirukkural — 1,330 couplets across 133 chapters of Aram, Porul, and Inbam — is one of the densest
ethical texts ever written, and most tools for it stop at lookup tables.

## Approach

A gamified Gen-AI product that answers questions about any couplet conversationally — in Tamil, English,
or Tanglish — built under Nitimis.

## Outcome

Shipped, with press coverage (Deccan Herald, Swarajya). My mother tongue and my day job finally shipped
something together.
