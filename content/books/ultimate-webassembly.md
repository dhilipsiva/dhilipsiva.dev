A practical guide to shipping WebAssembly where performance actually matters: compiling modules,
wiring Wasm to JavaScript and Rust, deploying to cloud and IoT, and tuning for production workloads.
Written by **Srinivas Shanmugam**, published by AVA (Orange Education) in 2026.

## My part

I was one of two technical reviewers (with Bhushan Nikhar). The job is unglamorous and load-bearing:
read every chapter adversarially, run the code, and flag the places where a claim is fluent but wrong
before it reaches print. Familiar instinct — it's the same one behind nibli, pointed at a manuscript
instead of a knowledge base.

Catching the fluent-but-wrong before it ships is the whole job, whether it's a book or an engine.
