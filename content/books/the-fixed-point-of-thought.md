A book on symbolic reasoning for builders — *The Fixed Point of Thought* is the working title; the real
one is still resolving.

## What it's about

How logic, recursion, and self-reference become real, running systems — and why a reasoning engine that
*derives* beats one that merely *predicts*. Twenty-two chapters, with [nibli](/things-i-built/nibli/) as
the central case study: compiling an unambiguous human language to first-order logic, backward chaining
with proof traces, and federating knowledge over peer-to-peer gossip. Equal parts logic textbook and lab
notebook.

## Where it's headed

The manuscript is one structural editing pass from ready. The ambition: neurosymbolic venues (NeSy),
formal-methods circles, arXiv, and an honest Show HN. If fluency and truth are different axes — and they
are — this book is about engineering the second one.
